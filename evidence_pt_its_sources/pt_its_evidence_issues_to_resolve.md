# Issues to Resolve Before Final Submission

1. **Comparable public-transport mode share is still unavailable for several secondary cities.**
   - The current dataset uses an ordinal PT supply score where mode-share data are absent.
   - This is acceptable for contextual diagnosis if clearly described as a proxy.

2. **Signal / ITS score is not a percentage.**
   - The editor asked about coordinated signal control systems. Comparable percentages were not publicly available for all 22 cities.
   - The current solution uses an ordinal 0–3 evidence-based score.

3. **Hat Yai has the weakest evidence.**
   - Current public transport evidence is based on local bus/van/songthaew/Happy Bus sources, not a strong official transport authority dataset.
   - Recommended follow-up: Hat Yai municipality or Songkhla provincial transport office.

4. **Several Malaysian secondary cities have conservative ITS scores.**
   - National ITS evidence exists, but city-specific coordinated signal evidence was not located for Ipoh, Johor Bahru, Kota Bharu, and Seberang Perai.
   - Do not overstate city-specific signal coordination for these cities.

5. **Hanoi ITS evidence is partly post-study-period.**
   - AI traffic-control-centre evidence is from 2026 sources and should be labelled as current management context, not as a 2024–2025 explanatory variable.

6. **Ho Chi Minh City ATO profile link issue.**
   - During collection, the ATO Ho Chi Minh profile link resolved to the Hanoi PDF. Therefore HCMC values use World Bank, HCMC Metro, SWARCO, and Vietnamnet sources instead.

7. **Boundary consistency still needs final wording.**
   - Several variables are metropolitan or functional-area proxies, especially Metro Manila, Kuala Lumpur/Kajang, Jakarta/Jabodetabek-related evidence, and Medan/Mebidang.
   - The manuscript should state that these are contextual proxies aligned to the nearest urban system, not exact administrative boundary measurements.
